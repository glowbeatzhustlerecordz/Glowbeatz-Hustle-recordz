Who We Are

More than a record label. A home for creativity.

GlowBeatz Hustle Records is a Zambian creative entertainment and talent development initiative built to identify, develop, empower and promote talented individuals.

We believe talent should not be wasted because of financial limitations, lack of exposure or limited opportunities. Every creative individual deserves an opportunity to grow, develop their craft and build a future through their talent.

Our platform brings together music, dance, performing arts, digital media, creative entrepreneurship and event experiences.

We don't just discover talent. We build potential into opportunity.

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading creative entertainment and talent development platforms.

Our Mission

To empower creative individuals through development, opportunities, exposure and entrepreneurship.

Our Values

Creativity, discipline, integrity, teamwork, empowerment and purposeful hustle.
