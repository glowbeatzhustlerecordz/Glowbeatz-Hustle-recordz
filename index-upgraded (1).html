More Than a Record Label

A home for talent, creativity and purposeful hustle.

GlowBeatz Hustle Records is a creative platform focused on discovering, developing, branding and promoting emerging talent. We bring creatives, audiences, organisations and opportunities together through entertainment, skills development, events and digital media. Our ambition is to help young people turn their creativity into meaningful careers and contribute to stronger communities.
